# Product

## Platform
web

## Audience
Students and families comparing education and career pathways, often on a phone and often before the student has a settled career identity.

## Purpose
Career Arbitrage helps a user discover which career tradeoffs they actually prefer before showing career labels. It makes time-to-income, education cost, debt, compensation, work style, schedule, pressure, portability and optionality visible without pretending to be a validated vocational assessment.

## Positioning
A decision simulator, not a personality quiz. The product should feel analytical, slightly irreverent and unusually honest about money.

## Evidence
The career dataset uses dated wage, tuition and program assumptions documented in DATA_NOTES.md and linked sources in the application. Results are compatibility estimates, not predictions.

## Brand commitments
- Never imply scientific validation that does not exist.
- Never hide assumptions in the wealth model.
- No account, telemetry, analytics or personal-data collection.
- Career titles remain hidden until the user completes the decision sequence.
- Optimize for a short mobile session without removing desktop functionality.
